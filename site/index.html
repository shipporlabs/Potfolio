FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is Potfolio financial advice?

No. The AI Guardian explains allocations and flags risks so you can learn. You make every decision and approve every action.

Can I buy and sell for real?

Yes. When you invest, Potfolio swaps USDC for tokenized stocks on Base through public exchanges, from your own wallet. You can sell back to USDC any time the market has liquidity. Each trade links to Basescan, and Potfolio covers the network fee.

What do I actually own?

Tokenized stocks issued on Base. Each is backed by a real share held by a regulated custodian and gives economic exposure, not voting rights.

How do I add money?

Pay by bank transfer or mobile money in your local currency through Light, or send USDC on Base straight to your wallet.

Who can use Potfolio?

Coinbase tokenized stocks are available only to eligible people outside the United States. Potfolio applies the same rule.

Where is my wallet key?

On your device. Potfolio creates a Base wallet on your phone or browser and never sends the key to our servers. Back it up from the Wallet tab.

Is there an iPhone app?

It’s coming to the App Store. Until then, open the web app in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. It installs like an app.
